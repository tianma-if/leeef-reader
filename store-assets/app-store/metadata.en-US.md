## Description

Leeef Reader is an offline-first, cross-platform ebook reader that keeps you in control of your data. Import and organize books without an account, then connect your own S3 or WebDAV storage when you want continuity across devices.

MULTI-FORMAT LIBRARY
Read EPUB, PDF, TXT, MOBI, AZW3, and FB2. Import from Files or the system share sheet, extract covers and metadata, and organize everything with shelves, tags, filters, and sorting.

FOCUSED READING
Enjoy interactive page curl on mobile, or choose slide, no animation, and continuous scrolling. Tune typography, spacing, margins, themes, dark mode, and Chinese text conversion.

HIGHLIGHTS, NOTES, AND INSIGHTS
Create bookmarks, highlights, and notes, review them by book or date, and export to Markdown, TXT, or CSV. Reading statistics help you understand time, streaks, and completion.

AI AND NARRATION, ON YOUR TERMS
Connect an AI provider you choose for explanations, translation, chapter summaries, and reading questions. Use system speech or a cloud voice provider. Your endpoint and API credentials are configured by you and kept in secure device storage.

YOUR CLOUD, YOUR DATA
Leeef stores data locally by default and requires no account. Optional S3 or WebDAV sync keeps books, progress, highlights, and shelves in step across iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.

Some online features require a network connection and third-party accounts or API credentials supplied by you.

## Review Notes

No account is required. Complete the three-page onboarding, tap Import Books, and select an EPUB, PDF, TXT, MOBI, AZW3, or FB2 file from Files. AI, cloud TTS, S3/WebDAV sync, and OPDS are optional and require third-party configuration supplied by the reviewer; they are not required for core offline reading.
