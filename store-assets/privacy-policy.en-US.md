# Leeef Reader Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026  
Effective date: August 26, 2026

Leeef Reader ("Leeef") is an offline-first ebook reader provided by Zhengzhou Binggui Network Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains how Leeef handles information.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Leeef does not require an account or sign-in and includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, or telemetry SDKs. We do not operate a Leeef cloud service that receives your books, reading activity, or notes, and we do not sell personal information.

By default, imported books and covers, shelves, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading statistics, settings, and app logs remain on your device.

## 2. Services You Choose to Enable

Data leaves your device only when you configure or use one of these features, and it is sent directly to the service you choose:

- **S3 or WebDAV sync:** book files, covers, reading progress, shelves, tags, bookmarks, highlights, notes, statistics, and sync metadata are uploaded to storage you provide.
- **AI assistant and translation:** submitted text, questions, and selected book or chapter context are sent to the AI provider you configure. Leeef supports user-provided API endpoints and keys.
- **Cloud text-to-speech:** text selected for narration is sent to the voice provider you configure. System text-to-speech is generally handled within operating-system capabilities.
- **OPDS and online catalogs:** search terms, catalog requests, and download requests are sent to the OPDS service you access.
- **External search, sharing, and export:** data is handled by the destination app, website, or system sharing service only when you initiate the action.

Those services are independently operated. Their privacy policies and your account settings govern their use, retention, and protection of data. Configure only services you trust.

## 3. Credentials and Security

API keys, storage credentials, and similar sensitive settings are kept in secure storage provided by the operating system. Network connections prefer HTTPS/TLS; end-to-end protection also depends on the service you configure. Protect your device, backups, and service credentials.

## 4. Permissions

Leeef may use network access, background tasks, audio playback, and notifications for downloads, synchronization, narration, and completion alerts. Leeef does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier permissions.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in Leeef, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You can delete books and related records, clear logs and caches, or remove external service settings. Data already synchronized to a third-party service must be deleted under that service's controls and policies.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Leeef is intended for general readers, is not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy and its date if features or data practices change. Material changes may also be announced in the app or store listing.
